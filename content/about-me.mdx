Yo what's up! My name is  Vincent, and I'm currently in my 2nd year studying Computer Science at the University of Waterloo in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada.

Growing up in Fredericton, New Brunswick, I spent lots of time playing sports, music, mountain biking, fishing, and watching beautiful sunsets with my friends. 
My love and drive for problem solving led me to where I am today at Waterloo. Learning, growing, making mistakes, and honestly just trying to push past my comfort zone by putting myself out there every single day, and I love it.

Now, I build and create fun full-stack applications, embedded systems, bugs (mostly unintentional), or things that make people go "Wow!", and I love what I do.
