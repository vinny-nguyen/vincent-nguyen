Hi there! I'm Vincent, and I'm currently a Computer Science student at the University of Waterloo.

Growing up in Fredericton, New Brunswick, I still vividly remember myself stuck in this weird lemma being a jack of all trades, 
but master of none type of situation. I was never the best at any sport, nor was I the most accomplished in academics, like alot of the kids I grew up with.
I grew up having some really close friends that I'd go biking or play music with, and that was a good chunk of my childhood.

Though, I didn't really know what I wanted to do with my life. 
I worked hard at school, and got some pretty good grades that led to me getting into Waterloo. 
But other than that, I wasn't special at anything.

I picked up Java through my Grade 12 Computer Science class, just because one of my friends was in it, 
that was my first ever exposure to programming.
I realized, it started to grow on me; gradually, slowly, but surely, and firmly reinforcing my long-lost love and drive for problem solving. 
As time went on, I just kept getting better and better, while still having fun and enjoying the learning process.

That's how I ended up here at Waterloo, still putting myself out there everyday in order to grow past my comfort zone, 
picking up new challenges and improving as I go on. Now, I build and design things that wow people, and I love what I do.
