What's up! I'm Vincent, and I'm currently in my 2nd year studying Computer Science at the University of Waterloo in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada.

Growing up in Fredericton, New Brunswick, I spent lots of time playing sports, piano, guitar, mountain biking, fishing, and watching beautiful sunsets with my friends. 
My love and drive for problem solving led me to where I am today at Waterloo. Learning, growing, making mistakes, putting myself out there, and pushing past my comfort zone every single day, while still enjoying the process.

Now, I build and create beautiful full-stack applications, embedded systems, bugs (mostly unintentional), things that make people go "Wow", and I love what I do.
